How it works

Nothing is fitted. The model is the weighted median weekly.wmedian with one change at the price cap.

  1. Cap. The cap of a day is the highest offer price over all supply curves of that day. The cap known for the delivery day D is the cap of day D-7.
  2. Cap mapping. In each of the 10 curves (the same interval 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 21 and 28 days earlier) whose own day has a cap different from the known cap, every step priced at or above the cap of its own day minus 0.5 $/MWh is set to the known cap. When the known cap is lower, every price above the known cap is also lowered to the known cap.
  3. Median. At each volume, the forecast price is the weighted median of the prices of the 10 mapped curves. The lag-7 curve has weight 2 and every other curve has weight 1. The forecast starts and ends where the lag-7 curve does.
